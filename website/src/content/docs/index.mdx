<h2 class="vc-section">Settings, without the desktop environment</h2>
<p class="vc-section-lead">The settings app GNOME and KDE users take for granted, for the desktops that leave it to you.</p>

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Network" icon="rocket">
    Wi-Fi, wired and VPNs: connect in a keystroke, and edit every setting, from WPA Enterprise to IPv6, DNS and routes. WireGuard and OpenVPN files, a hotspot, passwords in your keyring.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bluetooth" icon="setting">
    Pairing with every kind of code, battery levels, headset audio, sending and receiving files, and a phone's internet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sound" icon="seti:audio">
    The output and input in use, a volume per app and where it plays, ports, balance and profiles, live with PipeWire.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Displays" icon="laptop">
    Drag monitors into place, set modes, scale, HDR and VRR, and keep it or it goes back by itself. Workspaces on monitors, and profiles that switch when you plug in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your wallpaper's colours" icon="seti:image">
    Reads matugen palettes, ML4W's included, and recolours itself live when you change wallpaper. Five built-in themes otherwise.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Made for Hyprland" icon="approve-check">
    A normal window with the class <code>ch.giosuel.Lituus</code> for window rules, keyboard-driven (<kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> finds any setting), with a tray icon and quick popup. Its own Hyprland file, never yours.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

<h2 class="vc-section">Up and running in minutes</h2>
<p class="vc-section-lead">It uses what your system most likely runs already: NetworkManager, BlueZ, PipeWire and Hyprland.</p>

<div class="vc-steps">
  <div class="vc-step">
    <span class="vc-step__n">1</span>
    <h3>Install it</h3>
    <p>From the AUR, as a <code>.deb</code> or <code>.rpm</code>, or as an AppImage, for x86-64 and ARM64. See <a href="/install/">Installing</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vc-step">
    <span class="vc-step__n">2</span>
    <h3>Float it</h3>
    <p>Add a window rule and a keybind, so it opens where you want it. See <a href="/guide/hyprland/">Hyprland</a>.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="vc-step">
    <span class="vc-step__n">3</span>
    <h3>Set it up</h3>
    <p>Pick a network, pair your headphones, place your monitors. Lituus picks up what you already have; for Displays, add one line to your config (see <a href="/guide/displays/">Displays</a>).</p>
  </div>
</div>
